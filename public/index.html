Bio

YK

I build and run ERP systems, and I know shipping from the quay up.

Today I administer the JTL-Wawi ERP at RMtek, automate its workflows, build Python pipelines on top of its data, and run the company's webshop and eBay channel. Alongside that I'm finishing an M.Sc. in International Information Systems at FAU in Nuremberg.

Before that, I spent four and a half years at Hutchison Ports Pakistan, from top of my trainee batch to senior planner co-managing a team of six. I planned vessels of 2,000+ containers, tested new terminal operating systems, and helped bring a new terminal live in Jazan, Saudi Arabia.

That combination is what I bring: systems work grounded in how operations actually run, from ships and yards to warehouses and webshops.

Generation X and Y: Impact of Work Attitudes and Work Values on Employee Performance

Forecasts Pakistan's rising LNG imports and their impact on Port Qasim Authority, the only port receiving them: import volumes, LNG carrier traffic and the terminal capacity needed. Capstone project II · IoBM · 2023 · Group project

Application of Value Stream Mapping: A Case Study of an Automotive Industry

Ethnographic study of an exhaust-system assembly line at a Pakistani automotive parts manufacturer. Uses value stream mapping and lean tools (just-in-time, theory of constraints, Kanban, smaller lot sizes, 5S) to find waste and cut lead time, and recommends a layout change to reduce work in progress, scrap and labour costs while raising machine and labour utilisation.

IT and AI Self-efficacy: Success Attribution, Task Performance and Imposter Syndrome

Cross-sectional study of graduate students learning data analysis, testing four moderated research models with linear regression. AI self-efficacy showed a positive and stronger relationship with perceived task performance than IT self-efficacy, and imposter syndrome moderated the effect of IT self-efficacy.
