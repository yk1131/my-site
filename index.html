Bio

YK

I build and run ERP systems, and I know shipping from the quay up.

Today I run the JTL-Wawi ERP at RMtek, build Python automation around it, and keep the company's webshop and eBay channel working. Alongside that I'm finishing an M.Sc. in International Information Systems at FAU in Nuremberg.

Before that, I spent four and a half years at Hutchison Ports Pakistan, from trainee to senior planner. I planned vessels and yards, tested new terminal operating system releases, and helped configure a brand-new terminal in Jazan, Saudi Arabia.

That combination is where I'm heading: ERP for the shipping industry, built by someone who knows how ships, yards and warehouses actually work.
