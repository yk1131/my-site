About

My career started on the planning desk at Hutchison Ports Pakistan, where a good plan means ships leave on time and yard trucks don't queue. I worked my way from trainee to senior planner, joined a four-person special team planning for Yantian International Container Terminals in China, and helped bring a new terminal live in Jazan, Saudi Arabia.

That work taught me how much operations depend on the software underneath them, so I moved to Germany to study information systems. Today I run the ERP at RMtek, build automation around it in Python, and keep the company's webshop and eBay channel working.
